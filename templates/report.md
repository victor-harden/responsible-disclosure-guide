# Vulnerability report

**Title:**
**Reporter:**
**Date:**
**Affected asset / version:**

## Summary

Two or three sentences: what is wrong and what an attacker gains.

## Impact

Who is affected, what data or capability is at risk, and the conditions required.

## Reproduction

1. Preconditions (account, role, configuration).
2. Steps, with requests and responses.
3. Observed result.

## Evidence

Screenshots or logs with secrets redacted.

## Suggested remediation

The specific control that removes the weakness, plus a verification step.

## Disclosure preferences

Requested timeline, credit preference, contact method for follow-up.
